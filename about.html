About us

We believe arithmetic should be hard to get.

For decades, calculators simply gave people the answer. No process. No relationship. No recurring revenue. We founded MegaCalc to fix that.

Our story

Founded in a garage

MegaCalc began in 2019 in a suburban garage with three people, one whiteboard, and a shared conviction that the answer to 7 × 8 was worth considerably more than nothing.

The garage is still the data centre. We have grown to a team of four, including Gary, and we now serve over twelve thousand customers who are doing math right this second and have been for some time.

In 2024 we raised a Series C to secure exclusive commercial rights to the number 8. We are told the round was oversubscribed. We have not verified this, as it would require arithmetic.

Our values

  • Synergy. We are not sure what this means, but it is on the wall.
  • Velocity. We compute your answer in under a millisecond, then sit on it.
  • Radical transparency. We tell you the answer exists. Constantly.
  • Customer obsession. Gary will call you. Gary will keep calling you.
  • Not telling you the answer. This is the one we are best at.
